## Design Key Takeaways

### Dual-Chrome Navigation
The designs feature a stable top bar and bottom tab-bar. This "sandwich" layout anchors the user, providing constant access to core destinations (Home, Gallery, Contact) without needing to scroll back up or open a drawer.

### Thumb-Friendly Ergonomics
CTAs, filter chips, and primary action buttons (like "Next" in the wizard) are positioned at the bottom of the screen or in reachable "safe zones" for comfortable one-handed interaction.

### Native-Content-First Rhythm
The Home and Blog pages move away from "desktop cards centered on mobile" and instead use full-width blocks and immersive media rule-breaking to create a native app feel.
